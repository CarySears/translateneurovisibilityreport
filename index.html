Why it matters

A son or daughter worried about a parent’s memory often has questions before they are ready to call. If the only option is a phone call during office hours, many will keep searching.

AI assistants look for the same things. Our scan rated “send an enquiry” and “book a consultation” as not ready, while “find the phone number” and “start a call” were ready.

How fast that first reply comes decides whether you reach the family while they are still looking. Answering a new inquiry within an hour was nearly 7 times as likely to become a real conversation with the family member who can book as waiting another hour, and more than 60 times as likely as waiting 24 hours or longer.5 A call within 5 minutes was about 100 times more likely to reach that person than a call at 30 minutes, and about 21 times more likely to turn the call into a conversation that is ready to move forward.13 A promise of a reply within 24 hours sits at the slow end of that range, and a number that only rings gives a family no way to leave a message.

An AI voice agent can answer the phone 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. On the website, a voice and text concierge can answer immediately, on the same 24/7/365 schedule. Both can answer approved questions, book appointments and follow your escalation protocol. The setup is HIPAA compliant. A HIPAA-compliant web form can respond as soon as someone submits it, so a family is not left hoping for an email back. An embedded calendar lets them schedule a consultation on the site.
